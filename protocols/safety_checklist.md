# Safety checklist

Complete before every hardware session.

- [ ] Emergency stop / power cutoff is reachable.
- [ ] Follower base, leader base, cameras, and target area are mechanically secure.
- [ ] Cables cannot enter the arm workspace.
- [ ] Correct follower and leader serial devices are identified by stable paths.
- [ ] Correct calibration IDs/files are present; no recalibration is started implicitly.
- [ ] Both camera identities and orientations are verified.
- [ ] Pink cube, cyan cube, C1, and C2 are inside the conservative workspace.
- [ ] The neutral center target area holds both cubes without overlap.
- [ ] A slow manual reach check covers every layout and C1/C2.
- [ ] No person or fragile object is inside the motion envelope.
- [ ] First test uses conservative speed/relative-target limits approved by the operator.
- [ ] Operator knows: Right saves, Left re-records, Esc stops/finalizes.
