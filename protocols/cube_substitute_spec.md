# Household cube substitute specification

## Recommended construction

Make two rigid, visually plain cubes from identical small cardboard boxes or
identically cut dense foam cores.

- Starting side length: 40–45 mm.
- Use exactly the same geometry and approximately the same mass for both.
- Keep all faces flat, edges intact, and contents fixed so nothing shifts.
- Use the existing visually plain pink and cyan coverings consistently.
- Remove or cover text, logos, barcodes, pictures, and reflective surfaces.
- Use the same surface material and tape pattern on both cubes; only color may
  differ.
- Ensure both stand stably and do not collapse when lightly pinched.

The 40–45 mm size is a project starting recommendation, not an official SO-101
payload specification. Final acceptance requires a supervised low-risk
grasp/release check.

## Preferred household materials

1. Two identical small cardboard cube shells filled symmetrically with fixed
   paper or foam.
2. Two identically cut pieces of firm packaging foam, wrapped on all faces.
3. Two identical existing toy cubes with plain pink/cyan surfaces.

## Reject

- Cylinders, bottles, fruit, balls, toy vehicles, or irregular containers.
- Two objects with different size, mass, stiffness, texture, logos, or geometry.
- Empty cartons that slide/blow away easily.
- Soft sponges or plush objects that deform significantly.
- Glossy, transparent, metallic, patterned, or strongly reflective wrapping.
- Loose filling that changes the center of mass.

## Scene acceptance

- Both cubes are clearly pink/cyan in `front` and `side`.
- Neither color is confused with the gray interior or yellow boundary.
- Both cubes can be placed together at C1/C2 without overlap.
- One placed cube does not obstruct the second placement path.
- Cubes remain distinguishable when viewed next to each other and under current
  lighting.
- C1/C2 remain order-only positions and contain no pink/cyan color marker.
